# 0004: Launch date and page scope from the November 11 requirements session

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Deciders: Reed McIlwain, Nicole Peverley
- Spec references: sections 5, 7.1, 7.5, 7.7, 7.9, 8.2, 14; D4, D11, D13, D16, D19, D24, D26 to D30

## Context

Record 0002 set a mid-November target and listed Funding among the launch pages. A requirements session on November 11, 2026 set a firm date and changed what Home and the launch pages need. Where the session and spec v0.6 disagreed, the team chose the session's requirements.

## Options considered

1. **Keep spec v0.6.** One primary call to action (Contact us), Funding at launch, Truth and Reconciliation page in Phase 3.
2. **Adopt the session's requirements.** Update the spec to match.

## Decision

Option 2.

- Launch target is November 25, 2026, with copy ready for joint review by November 21 (D16).
- Funding moves to post-MVP and becomes a designed "wall of funding" (section 7.5).
- Home becomes a sequence of full-width sections with two calls to action above the fold: teachers, and students and parents (D4, section 7.1).
- An Indigenous outreach page replaces any land acknowledgement and moves toward launch, pending board confirmation (D13, section 7.9).
- Privacy may open as a dialog, with `/privacy` kept as the route (section 7.7).
- The page receipt becomes a stretch goal for launch (section 8.2).
- Board and Contact stay in the launch pages.
- D19 holds for launch. Open questions are recorded as D26 to D30.

## Consequences

- Home needs more sections and a teacher destination (D26) before launch.
- Issues #7 (Home) and #11 (Funding) need their acceptance criteria updated.
- The board still needs to confirm the outreach page (D13) and funding disclosure (D11).
