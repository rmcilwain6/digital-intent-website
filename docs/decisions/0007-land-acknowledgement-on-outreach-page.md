# 0007: A land acknowledgement on the Indigenous outreach page

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Deciders: [TK: Reed McIlwain and the team member leading Indigenous outreach approve in the pull request; board confirmation if needed]
- Spec references: section 7, section 7.1, section 7.9, D13, R6, R7, section 12.5. Replaces the D13 part of record 0004.

## Context

Record 0004 (November 11, 2026) said the Indigenous outreach page would replace any land acknowledgement, and the spec said there would be no land acknowledgement on Home, in the footer or anywhere else (D13). The aim was to do more than a statement.

A conversation on November 24, 2026 with the team member leading this work changed the view. Until we have more to show for our work with Indigenous schools and communities, a land acknowledgement is a good-faith place to start. The outreach page is how we go beyond it, not a reason to leave it out.

## Options considered

1. Keep D13 as written: no acknowledgement anywhere, the outreach page only. With little to show yet, the page would say less than a plain acknowledgement does.
2. An acknowledgement in the footer only. Common, and easy to make performative.
3. A full acknowledgement on the outreach page, and a short one in the footer that links to it. The page then carries our approach, our commitments and a record of what we have done.

## Decision

Option 3.

- `/indigenous-outreach/` holds the full acknowledgement: the Nations for each starting region (Kamloops, Victoria, Vancouver), and the First Nations across the whole province, because our work is province-wide. The Nations are named by the team member leading this work, not from a dataset.
- Every page's footer carries a short acknowledgement with a link to the outreach page. The page stays out of the primary navigation until its copy is approved.
- The page map starts empty. A school becomes a point once we have visited or worked with it, with the school's agreement. Points are schools, not Nation boundaries. We do not load a list of First Nations schools to fill the map in (R6).
- Copy may be drafted from the lead's notes, and needs that person's approval before launch (section 12.5).

## Consequences

- Wording must be accurate for every region named. "Unceded" does not fit all of BC: Victoria is in Douglas Treaty territory, the northeast is under Treaty 8, and several Nations have modern treaties.
- The footer acknowledgement appears on every page, so its wording needs the same approval as the page.
- An empty map is honest but plain at first. It fills in only as fast as real relationships do.
- Revisit when the lead or the board wants a different form, or when the page has enough of a record that the acknowledgement should change.
