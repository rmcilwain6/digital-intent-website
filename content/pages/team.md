We are a small, volunteer, remote-first team based in Kamloops, Victoria and Vancouver.

We're a team of young people who grew up alongside the technology that is now reshaping our world. We see a growing gap between that world and how ready students are for it, so we came together to help close it, for students and for the teachers who support them.
